What this application does and does not do
Does: answers common manager questions using guidance grounded in real RealPage HR documents (Requisition Intake, Talent Reviews & UKG Playbook, Change Management Toolkit, Performance Conversation guides, the Employee Handbook, Benefits Summary, New Leader Toolkit, and more), cites the real source document for each answer, and hands back a checklist and an escalation point. The People Snapshot uses real, single-column aggregate counts from the sponsor's de-identified 10_Sample HR Dataset export, served from the .NET API's data service. The Leadership Update example and Adoption & Impact usage numbers remain clearly labeled illustrative/synthetic.

Does not: use any individual employee's private data, and never makes a pay, performance, legal, employment, or production decision. Anything in that territory routes to a named human reviewer.
