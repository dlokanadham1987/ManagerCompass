# Q3 AI Hackathon participant package

This package helps Q3 hackathon teams work effectively during the 48-hour event without providing a use-case answer or preferred solution.

## Start here

1. Read `00_Quick_Start.docx`.
2. Read `01_Rules_Guardrails_and_Fair_Play.docx` before using tools, connectors, data, or reusable assets.
3. Set up the shared Teams-connected SharePoint workspace with `02_Global_Collaboration_and_Shift_Handoffs.docx`.
4. Use `03_48_Hour_Team_Charter_and_Plan.docx` to record roles, working hours, scope, and handoffs.
5. Use `04_Build_Test_and_Evidence_Guide.docx` to record evidence in `STATUS.md`.
6. Use `05_Demo_and_Submission_Guide.docx` and the final checklist before posting the final package.
7. Give `07_Participant_Agent_Context.md` to ChatGPT/Codex or Claude with the assigned brief and the team's specific task.

Build work begins Wednesday, September 16, 2026. The team Teams channel is the source for any day-of kickoff details, the current meeting link, shared FAQ, sponsor or SME route, and event updates.

The 48-hour window does not require continuous or overnight work. Plan reasonable local working hours, overlap time, and handoffs in the team charter.

## Before kickoff: prepare, do not build

- You may read the assigned brief, confirm access, create the shared folder structure, copy templates, assign roles, list time zones and working hours, and record questions.
- Do not create or refine a submission solution, work with sponsor data, or use a prebuilt sponsor-specific answer before the official kickoff.
- A generic template or prior team-owned asset is acceptable only when the team has permission to use it, it does not contain an existing solution to the assigned use case, the assigned brief allows it, and the final README identifies what was reused and what was created during the event.

Copy these into `00_Admin/`:

- `HANDOFF.md`

Copy these into `03_Final_Submission/`:

## How judging connects to the package

Judges will consider business value, practicality, clarity of the prototype, adoption potential, follow-through, workflow quality, and safety and governance. Teams should show evidence tied to the assigned use-case brief; polish or novelty alone is not enough.

Judging runs Friday, September 18 through Friday, September 25, 2026. Winners will be announced during the September 30, 2026 Town Hall.

## Core rules

- Teams have three to five people.
- Use Realpage AI-licensed tools such as Copilot, RPGPT, ChatGPT/Codex, and Claude when your team has access.
- Use managed connectors already available in those systems. Do not create, install, or configure a custom MCP server.
- Browser and computer use are allowed within approved data, permission, and tool boundaries.
- Use sponsor-provided material or data your team owns or controls and is permitted to use. Confirm separately that you may access it, use it in the specific approved tool, and include or show it in the final package.
- Never place credentials, API keys, secrets, or access tokens in a prompt, connector, repository, or shared file.
- Do not make or automate customer, employee, employment, compensation, performance, legal, or production decisions or actions. Keep a human reviewer in the loop and document the review point.
- For people-related work, use approved HR guidance and non-sensitive organization-level or aggregated data. Do not use individual employee data to make employment, compensation, performance, or legal decisions.
- Keep working files and the final package in the team Teams channel's connected SharePoint workspace.
- Choose the output format that fits the use case, while satisfying the assigned brief, evidence requirements, and confirmed scope. A video does not replace an underlying artifact that the brief requires.

## Support and final-package access

For the use case, data, or business decision, start with the use-case SME directory above and the current assigned brief or team channel. If no contact or support window is available, record the question and safe fallback in `STATUS.md`, then ask event support to route it.

For a Teams-channel, shared-file, reviewer-access, submission-location, or event-routing problem, contact `Austin.braham@realpage.com`.

At the deadline, stop modifying the final package. If an outage or access issue occurs, record the time and affected item, keep any permitted fallback evidence in the Teams channel, and contact Austin immediately. The event team decides whether a correction is allowed; do not assume an extension.
